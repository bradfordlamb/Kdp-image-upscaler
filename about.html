About

kdpimageupscaler.com is run by Brad Lamb. He is a Kindle Direct Publishing author and publishes under the name Maxwell Pepper.

The site is a free browser tool for preparing images before a KDP upload, plus a set of guides on print resolution, cover size, and file format. You open a PNG, JPEG, WEBP, BMP, or GIF on the upscaler. The page resamples it to a larger pixel grid and downloads the result. The image stays on your device. The privacy policy describes that path and the advertising cookies used on the site.

The guides summarize KDP's published image and cover rules and explain how this particular tool behaves, including its limits. They are not Amazon's documentation. Trim sizes, spine formulas, and file rules change. The KDP Help pages linked from each guide are the source to check before you submit a book.

This site is not affiliated with Amazon.com, Inc. or with Kindle Direct Publishing. "KDP" and "Kindle" are trademarks of Amazon.com, Inc. Cover Creator, Cover Studio, and the KDP cover calculator are Amazon's tools. This site does not replace them.

The upscaler is a canvas resample, not a generative model. It can raise the pixel count so a file meets a DPI target at a chosen print size. It cannot restore focus or detail that was not in the original.
