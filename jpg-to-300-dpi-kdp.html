KDP Cover Size Calculator Guide

A KDP cover needs the right physical dimensions and the right pixel dimensions. If either one is wrong, your cover can look blurry, stretched, or misaligned.

Use the Free KDP Image Upscaler Tool

The basic formula

Pixels = inches × DPI. If your cover image needs to print 6 inches wide at 300 DPI, it should be at least 1,800 pixels wide.

Do not forget bleed and spine

Full paperback covers include the front cover, back cover, spine, and bleed. The final size depends on trim size, page count, paper type, and bleed settings.

Best workflow

Calculate the required cover dimensions, export the cover at high resolution, then use an upscaler only if the image does not meet the needed pixel size.
